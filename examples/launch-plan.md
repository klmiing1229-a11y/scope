# Example: a wish that is really two projects

**User:** scope launch my online shop and a brand and a newsletter

**Scope asks (one message):**
1. Which one first? a) the shop (default) b) the brand c) the newsletter
2. What does done look like? (default) a customer can find one product and pay for it
3. Limits? (default) free or cheap tools, ready in 2 weeks

**User:** ok

**Brief:**

**Wish:** launch my online shop and a brand and a newsletter
**Scoped as:** a one-product online shop that can take a real order.

**For:** first customers from your own network
**Done when:**
- One product page with photo, price and description is live
- A test order completes from basket to payment confirmation
- The shop link works on a phone

**Limits:** cheap tools, 2 weeks
**Start from:** from scratch
**Out of scope (for now):** brand identity, newsletter, more than one product
**Assumed:** the product and the payment method are already chosen

**First step:** pick the shop platform and list the one product's name, price and photo.

Run it? (ok / change X / just the brief)

*(The wish was three projects. `scope` took the first and parked the other two instead of planning all three.)*
